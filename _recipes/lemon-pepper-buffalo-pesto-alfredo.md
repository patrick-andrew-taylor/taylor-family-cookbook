Four loud ideas in one pan, still arguing about who is in charge.

> **Work in progress.** The balance between the pesto and the buffalo sauce is the open question -- suggestions very welcome.

## Ingredients

### Chicken

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|:-:|:-:|
| Boneless Skinless Chicken Thighs[^1] | 1 1/2 lb (680 g) |
| Lemon-Pepper Seasoning | 2 Teaspoons |
| Kosher Salt | 1/2 Teaspoon |
| Olive Oil | 1 Tablespoon |

### Sauce

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|:-:|:-:|
| Unsalted Butter | 4 Tablespoons |
| Garlic Cloves | 4, Minced |
| Black Pepper | 1 Teaspoon, Coarsely Cracked |
| Heavy Cream | 1 1/2 Cups |
| Buffalo Sauce[^2] | 1/3 Cup |
| Basil Pesto[^3] | 1/4 Cup |
| Parmigiano-Reggiano | 1 Cup, Finely Grated |
| Lemon | 1, Zested, Plus 1 Tablespoon Juice |
| Lemon-Pepper Seasoning | 1 Teaspoon |

### Pasta

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|:-:|:-:|
| Fettuccine | 1 lb (454 g) |
| Reserved Pasta Water | 1 1/2 Cups |

### To Finish

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|:-:|:-:|
| Crumbled Blue Cheese *or* Torn Basil[^4] | To Taste |
| Parmigiano-Reggiano | To Taste |
| Lemon Zest | To Taste |

## Instructions

1. Salt a large pot of water heavily and bring to a boil. Toss the chicken thighs with the lemon-pepper seasoning and kosher salt.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear the thighs 5-6 minutes per side, until deeply browned and 175°F (79°C) internal. Move to a cutting board to rest. Do not wipe out the pan.
3. Cook the fettuccine to 1 minute under package al dente. **Reserve 1 1/2 cups of pasta water**, then drain.
4. Melt the butter in the chicken pan over medium heat, scraping up the browned bits. Add the garlic and cracked pepper and cook 45 seconds, until fragrant -- do not let the garlic brown.
5. Pour in the heavy cream and simmer gently 4-5 minutes, until it coats the back of a spoon.[^5]
6. **Take the pan off the heat.** Whisk in the buffalo sauce, then the pesto, then the Parmigiano-Reggiano in three additions, until glossy.[^6]
7. Finish the sauce with the lemon zest, lemon juice, and the remaining teaspoon of lemon-pepper seasoning. Taste and adjust.[^7]
8. Slice the chicken against the grain. Return the skillet to low heat, add the drained pasta and a splash of pasta water, and toss hard for a minute until the sauce clings. Add more water a tablespoon at a time -- it will look too loose right before it looks right.
9. Plate with the chicken on top, extra Parmigiano-Reggiano, lemon zest, and your chosen garnish.

> It should read hot first, then bright, with the basil arriving last.

[^1]: Thighs hold up to the sauce better than breast does.
[^2]: Frank's Red Hot *Wing Sauce*, not plain hot sauce. The homemade [Buffalo Sauce](buffalo-sauce.md) works too, and is richer -- cut the butter in this recipe to 3 tablespoons if you use it.
[^3]: Pesto is a seasoning here, not the sauce. Cheap jarred pesto tastes like grass clippings under this much dairy.
[^4]: Pick one. Blue cheese pulls the dish toward wings, basil pulls it toward Italian. Both at once tastes confused.
[^5]: Keep it at a lazy bubble. A hard boil here is what makes it break later.
[^6]: Off the heat matters -- the vinegar in the buffalo sauce plus boiling cream and cheese is exactly how this curdles.
[^7]: Lemon-pepper blends vary wildly in salt, so taste before adding any more.
